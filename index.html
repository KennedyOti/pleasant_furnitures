About Pleasant Furnitures

Founded in 2010, Pleasant Furnitures has grown to become one of Kenya's premier furniture design and manufacturing companies. Our passion for craftsmanship and attention to detail sets us apart.

We combine traditional woodworking techniques with modern design principles to create furniture that is both beautiful and built to last. All our products are made in Kenya using sustainably sourced materials.
